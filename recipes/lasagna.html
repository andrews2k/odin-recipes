Lasagna

Description

Classic homemade lasagna layered with rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and plenty of mozzarella cheese. Perfect comfort food for family dinners.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions; drain and set aside.
  3. Brown ground beef with onion and garlic in a skillet over medium heat. Drain fat.
  4. Stir marinara sauce into the beef mixture; simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Mix ricotta, egg, salt, and pepper together in a bowl.
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9×13 inch baking dish.
  7. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat layers twice.
  8. Top with remaining mozzarella and Parmesan.
  9. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake 15 minutes more.
  10. Let rest 10 minutes before serving.

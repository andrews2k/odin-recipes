Pancakes

Description

Light and fluffy classic buttermilk pancakes. Quick to make and always a crowd-pleaser for breakfast or brunch. Serve with maple syrup and butter.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together buttermilk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined (lumps are okay).
  4. Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly grease with butter.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake onto the griddle.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look set, about 2–3 minutes.
  7. Flip and cook until golden brown on the other side, about 1–2 minutes.
  8. Serve immediately with maple syrup and butter.
